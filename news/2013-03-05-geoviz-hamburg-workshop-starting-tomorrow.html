GeoViz Hamburg workshop starting tomorrow

Sebastian Pasewaldt and Stefan Buschmann will leave to Hamburg today. They will present their current work on Thursday at the GeoViz 2013 workshop.

Stefan Buschmann will talk about "Towards Visualizing Geo-Referenced Climate Networks" on Thursday, at 14:15, in Session 8 and Sebastian presents "Potentials of and Approaches for Multi-Perspective Visualization of 3D Spatial Models" on the same day, at 12:05, in Session 7.
