Two Contributions to EUROGRAPHICS'2021

Two contributions were accepted for the EUROGRAPHICS'2021:

  • "Interactive Photo Editing on Smartphones via Intrinsic Decomposition​​​" (Project Site)
    Sumit Shekhar, Max Reimann, Maximilian Mayer, Amir Semmo, Sebastian Pasewaldt, Jürgen Döllner, and Matthias Trapp
  • "Teaching Data-driven Video Processing via Crowdsourced Data Collection"
    Max Reimann, Ole Wegen, Sebastian Pasewaldt, Amir Semmo, Jürgen Döllner, and Matthias Trapp

The conference is taking place virtually 3–7 May: https://conferences.eg.org/eg2021/. This research was enabled by the collaboration of Digital Masterpieces and the Computer Graphics Systems Group (funded by the German Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF) through grant 01IS18092/"mdViPro" and 01IS19006/"KI-LAB-ITSE").
