Best Paper and Best Demo awards at SIGGRAPH Asia MGIA

Tobias Dürschmid, Maximilian Söchting, Amir Semmo, Matthias Trapp, and Jürgen Döllner have received the Best Paper Award for "ProsumerFX: Mobile Design of Image Stylization Components" at the SIGGRAPH Asia 2017 Symposium on Mobile Graphics and Interactive Applications (MGIA). At the same symposium, Sebastian Pasewaldt, Amir Semmo, Mandy Klingbeil, and Jürgen Döllner also received the Best Demo Award for their contribution "Pictory - Neural Style Transfer and Editing with CoreML". SIGGRAPH Asia 2017 was held in Bangkok, Thailand from the 27th to 30th November.
