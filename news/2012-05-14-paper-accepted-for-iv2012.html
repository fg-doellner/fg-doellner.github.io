Paper accepted for IV2012

IV2012, the 16th International Conference on Information Visualisation, did accept a paper on "A Visual Analysis Approach to Support Perfective Software Maintenance" by Jonas Trümper, Martin Beck, and Jürgen Döllner. IV2012 will be held on July 10-13, 2012, in Montpellier, France.
