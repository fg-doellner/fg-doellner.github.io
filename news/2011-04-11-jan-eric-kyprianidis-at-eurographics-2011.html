Jan Eric Kyprianidis at Eurographics 2011

Jan Eric Kyprianidis is joining the Eurographics 2011, which is held from April 11-15, 2011, in Llandudno, Wales UK. He is not only contributing a full paper entitled "Image and Video Abstraction by Coherence-Enhancing Filtering" together with Henry Kang but is also contributing a tutorial about "Artistic Stylisation of Images and Video" in cooperation with John Collomosse.
