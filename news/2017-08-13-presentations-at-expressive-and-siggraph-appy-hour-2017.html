Presentations at Expressive and SIGGRAPH Appy Hour 2017

On July 29th 2017, Amir Semmo presented the paper "Neural Style Transfer: A Paradigm Shift for Image-based Artistic Rendering?" at the joint Expressive Symposium (CAe, NPAR, SBIM), which was held in Los Angeles, CA, USA. The paper is a joint work of the research group with Tobias Isenberg from Team AVIZ–Inria-Saclay. The presentation slides are available here.

In addition, Mandy Klingbeil and Amir Semmo presented on Aug 2nd 2017 the demo "Pictory: Combining Neural Style Transfer and Image Filtering" at SIGGRAPH Appy Hour. More information on the public release of "Pictory" is available at the website of the project partner Digital Masterpieces.
