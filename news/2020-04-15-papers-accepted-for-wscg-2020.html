Papers accepted for WSCG 2020

The paper "Intermediate Representations for Vectorization of Stylized Images" by Daniel-Amadeus Johannes Glöckner, Lisa Ihde, Jürgen Döllner, and Matthias Trapp and the paper"Real-time Service-based Stream-processing of High-resolution Videos" by Florian T. Wagner, Jürgen Döllner, and Matthias Trapp have been accepted as contributions to the 28. International Conference in Central Europe on Computer Graphics, Visualization and Computer Vision 2020 at Plzen, Czech Republic.

Link to the conference website: http://www.wscg.cz
